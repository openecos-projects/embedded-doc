# StarrySky C Board
## Board Description
This page is the home page of the **development documentation** for the StarrySky C single-board computer. The StarrySky C series single-board computer is an official board specially planned and designed by the project team in August 2025, building on the design experience accumulated from previous iterations of StarrySky L boards and other practical projects such as test boards. It is based on **open-source tape-out SoCs**, and is **mainly intended to undertake comprehensive evaluation, embedded software adaptation, prototyping, and other functions for community-commissioned tape-out SoC chips.** By building a brand-new StarrySky C single-board computer brand, the project team hopes to provide a stable and reliable hardware carrier for the open-source chip ecosystem, thereby accelerating the transformation from chip design to practical application. In addition, besides the officially released StarrySky C boards, the project team plans in the future to distribute some already packaged chips to users who participated in the tape-out shuttle or to core contributors of the embedded community, supporting them in designing their own chip boards to further extend the functions of the official boards.

## Versioning Convention
Currently, the hardware version naming of StarrySky development boards follows the format: **StarrySky C board + V major version.minor version**. A change in the **major version** indicates a **generational** change in the open-source tape-out SoC carried, such as an evolution of the SoC architecture or an improvement in the process node. A change in the **minor version** indicates a modification of the board's hardware peripherals.
